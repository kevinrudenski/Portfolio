# Excel Financial Models

Three Excel projects showing financial modeling and dashboard work.

Three Statement Model: a five year forecast where the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow are fully linked and tie out from one page of assumptions.

Multifamily Acquisition Model: underwriting for a 40 unit apartment deal, from assumptions through the sale, landing on IRR, equity multiple, and cash on cash, with a sensitivity table.

Vacation Rental Dashboard: a profit and loss dashboard across 300 properties, with the best and worst performers by profit and by guest rating.
